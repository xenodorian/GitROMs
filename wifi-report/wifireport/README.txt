WiFi report (read-only)
=======================
1. Extract this zip into the ports folder on the roms partition.
   You get ports/WiFiReport.sh and ports/wifireport/.
2. Plug the Wi-Fi dongle into the handheld first, then open
   Ports > WiFiReport. Wait about 10 seconds for the message.
3. Power off, put the card in a PC, and send ports/wifireport/WiFi_Report.txt.

It changes nothing and installs nothing. It records no Wi-Fi names or
passwords and masks MAC and IP addresses.
